How it works
  1. Script pre-review (when a script and client instructions are supplied): the local AI checks for conflicts before video analysis. Its suggested checks guide later AI review; verify them against the finished video.
  2. Automatic checks (run on your PC, nothing is uploaded to the internet): dead air, blank clips, glitches and freezes, blurry images, audio problems, low-contrast text, spelling of on-screen text, and whether every year, percentage, measure and name the narrator says is highlighted on screen at the right time.
  3. You review: play the video, click any flag to jump to it, then mark it Confirm problem or Not a problem and type a note.
  4. Judgment checks (scene pacing, delayed overlays, wrong footage, maps, aesthetics): review the flags and use AI suggest Notes / fix on an issue for an editable suggestion. Verify it against the video.
  5. Narrator: pause on a frame showing the narrator and click "This frame shows the narrator". The app finds every shot he appears in.
  6. Export a Word report with timecodes, thumbnails, your notes and the full transcript.
